Analog Repair
=============

Everything needed is in this folder: the app, the video engine and FFmpeg.
Nothing else has to be installed.

Start
-----
Windows : double-click AnalogRepair.Desktop.exe
          (SmartScreen may ask once: "More info" -> "Run anyway")
Linux   : AppImage: chmod +x Analog-Repair-*.AppImage, then start it
          tar.gz:   ./AnalogRepair.Desktop
macOS   : open "Analog Repair.app"
          The app is not notarised. The first time, right-click it and choose
          "Open", or run:  xattr -cr "Analog Repair.app"

Use
---
1 Import  - drop your digitised tapes or film into the window
2 Trim    - split at the playhead (S), click a part, Delete removes it;
            drag the [ ] edges to trim, Space plays the kept parts
3 Repair  - choose Tape or Film, adjust the sliders, tick "Remove date
            stamp" if needed, "Render this frame" to check
4 Export  - choose a folder and start; remaining time is shown live.
            Afterwards: original and result side by side, plus an estimate
            of what was gained and what is still possible without loss.
            (folder, settings and cuts are remembered for next time)
